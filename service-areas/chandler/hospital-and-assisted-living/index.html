Do you travel to Chandler for hospital and assisted-living visits?
Yes — Chandler is inside the standard service area, covering 85224, 85225, 85226, 85248, 85249, 85286. Chandler is a straight run down the 202 or Arizona Avenue, and evening appointments here are routine. Travel is quoted before the appointment is booked.
Can you notarize for someone in the hospital who is heavily medicated?
Only if they are alert enough to understand what they are signing and to say clearly that they want to sign it. Medication does not automatically rule it out, but confusion, sedation or an inability to communicate does. If that is the situation on arrival, the appointment is rescheduled rather than forced.
What if the patient has no ID with them?
Arizona allows a credible witness to identify a signer: someone who personally knows them, who has their own valid ID, and who has no stake in the document. Bringing that person along is often faster than sending someone home for a wallet.
How soon can somebody be in Chandler?
Same-day is usually possible, and after-hours appointments are arranged where the situation calls for it. 7am–9pm weekdays, 8am–8pm weekends, with after-hours and emergency appointments by arrangement.
