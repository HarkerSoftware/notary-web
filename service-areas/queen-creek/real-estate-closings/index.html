Questions

Do you travel to Queen Creek for real estate closings?
Yes — Queen Creek is inside the standard service area, covering 85140, 85142, 85143. Queen Creek and San Tan Valley are the far edge of the service area, so same-day appointments here are best arranged in the morning. Travel is quoted before the appointment is booked.
Can you explain what I am signing?
No. Explaining the effect of a closing document is the work of your escrow officer, lender or attorney, and a notary who does it is practising law without a licence. The appointment can wait while you call them.
Do both owners have to be present?
Every person whose signature is being notarized has to be physically present with their own ID. They do not have to be present at the same time, but a single appointment is usually simpler than two.
How soon can somebody be in Queen Creek?
Same-day is usually possible, and after-hours appointments are arranged where the situation calls for it. 7am–9pm weekdays, 8am–8pm weekends, with after-hours and emergency appointments by arrangement.
