Mobile notary services

Each of these travels to you anywhere in Gilbert, Chandler, Mesa, Queen Creek and Apache Junction. The pages below cover what to bring, what the appointment involves, and the rules that decide whether a document can actually be notarized on the day.

Hospital and assisted-living visits

When somebody is admitted, the paperwork that nobody got around to becomes urgent overnight. A bedside visit is the most common call a mobile notary takes, and it is the one where showing up quickly and knowing the rules matters most.

Hospital and Assisted-Living Notary Visits

Estate planning documents

An estate plan is rarely one document. It is a stack of them, several of which need a notary and some of which need witnesses too, and it is much easier to do the stack in one sitting than to chase signatures over three weeks.

Estate Planning Document Notarization

Power of attorney

A power of attorney is the document families most often need in a hurry and most often get wrong, because Arizona asks for more than a notary stamp on the financial one.

Power of Attorney Notarization

Wills and trusts

A will and a trust are signed differently, and the difference catches people out. One usually needs two witnesses and a notarized affidavit; the other usually needs the notary directly.

Will and Trust Notarization

Real estate closings

Not every closing happens at a title company's conference table. Sellers who have already moved, buyers with a shift that ends at seven, and out-of-state parties all need somebody to bring the table to them.

Mobile Real Estate Closing Notary

Loan signings

A loan package is a hundred-odd pages with a dozen places where a wrong date or a missed initial sends the whole thing back. A signing agent's job is to get through it accurately, at the borrower's kitchen table, on the schedule escrow set.

Mobile Loan Signing Agent

Apostille assistance

A document going abroad usually needs more than a notary stamp: it needs the state to certify that the notary was real. That certification is an apostille, and getting one is a courier job with rules that are easy to get wrong.

Apostille Assistance

Vehicle and title documents

Arizona title work is one of the few everyday errands that still needs a notary, and it always seems to come up on a Saturday when the buyer is standing in the driveway with cash.

Vehicle Title and MVD Notary

Same-day and after-hours service

Notarizations become urgent at inconvenient times, which is most of the reason mobile notaries exist. Evening, weekend and short-notice work is normal here rather than an exception grudgingly made.

Same-Day and After-Hours Mobile Notary
